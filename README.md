## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Retail Analytics Dashboard Suite developed using SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Tableau Public using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow including data preparation, SQL-based business analysis, KPI development, and dashboard creation to generate actionable business insights.

The final solution consists of four executive-level dashboards designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, regional trends, discount impact, and product performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail organizations generate large volumes of transactional data across products, regions, and customer segments. Without proper analysis, identifying profit drivers, loss-making products, and regional performance trends becomes difficult.

This project aims to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards that support strategic decision-making.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### Executive Insights

- Technology emerged as the highest-performing category in terms of both sales and profit.
- Furniture received the highest average discount but generated significantly lower profit.
- Higher discounts showed a negative impact on overall profitability.

### Regional Insights

- The West region generated the highest overall profit.
- The Central region was the least profitable region.
- Several states generated losses despite maintaining healthy sales volumes.

### Customer Insights

- Consumer customers contributed over 50% of total sales.
- Standard Class was identified as the most profitable shipping mode.
- Home Office customers achieved the highest profit margins.

### Product Insights

- Copiers emerged as the most profitable sub-category.
- Tables were identified as the largest loss-making sub-category.
- Phones generated the highest sales revenue.
